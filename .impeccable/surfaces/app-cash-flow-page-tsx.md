# Cash Flow surface brief

- Primary target: `app/cash-flow/page.tsx`
- Related targets: `components/cash-flow/CashFlowOverview.tsx`, `components/cash-flow/CashFlowOverview.module.css`
- Mode: Operate
- Thesis: A household retention story—money in, money out, and the savings momentum between them.
- Hierarchy: current net cash flow first; income/spending/savings rate integrated into its plane; monthly comparison and savings momentum next; categories, goals, and projections deepen the explanation.
- Spending source: derived from Budget category purchases, never a duplicate transaction ledger.
- Income source: atomic income entries summed automatically in a desktop side drawer or mobile bottom drawer.
- Material: selective glass only on the cash-flow hero and compact contextual controls; analytical panels remain opaque.
- Motion: charts render immediately; entry actions favor direct feedback; drawer motion supplies spatial context.
