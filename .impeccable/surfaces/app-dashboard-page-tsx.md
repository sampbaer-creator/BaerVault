## Scope and mode

Dashboard route (`/dashboard`), Operate mode.

## Audience and job

A household member needs to understand the household's financial position now, spot whether the month is on track, and know where to investigate next.

## Content and constraints

Net worth is dominant; income, spending, budget remaining, and investments are secondary. Budget, portfolio, and recent activity deepen the overview without becoming equal-weight cards. Data is illustrative mock data. Existing desktop and mobile shell behavior remains intact.

## Direction and memorable moment

Origin-quality personal-finance hierarchy translated into BearVault navy, muted green, and vault-like stability. Restrained Liquid Glass appears on the dominant net-worth surface and compact controls; detail panels remain opaque. The memorable moment is one calm, protected financial horizon: net worth, trend, and household summary read as a single surface.
